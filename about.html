Who We Are

REVA University is a private university located in Bengaluru, Karnataka. It provides education in various fields and encourages students to develop their knowledge, skills and creativity.

Our Vision

To become a leading institution that creates responsible professionals and contributes to society through education and innovation.

Our Mission

To provide quality education, promote research and create opportunities for students to achieve their goals.
